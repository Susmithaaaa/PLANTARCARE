Foot Care & Footwear

Choose comfortable and supportive footwear to help protect your feet during daily activities.

👟

Comfortable Fit

Select footwear that fits comfortably and provides enough space for the toes without excessive pressure.

🦶

Suitable Support

Choose footwear that provides appropriate cushioning and support for the heel and arch during everyday activities.

✓

Avoid Excessive Wear

Check footwear regularly and replace shoes when cushioning or support has become significantly worn.

🚶

Activity-Based Choice

Use comfortable footwear that is appropriate for your walking, standing, exercise, or other daily activities.
