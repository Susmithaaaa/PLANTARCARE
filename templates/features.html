PROJECT FEATURES

Smart Risk Assessment

PLANTARCARE combines assessment inputs, BMI analysis and Random Forest prediction into an educational plantar fasciitis risk assessment workflow.

Key Project Features

01

BMI Analysis

Calculates Body Mass Index using the user's height and weight.

02

Plantar Pain

Records the user's plantar pain response during the assessment.

03

Morning Pain

Collects information about pain experienced during the first steps in the morning.

04

Standing Hours

Includes the user's daily standing duration as an assessment input.

05

Walking Activity

Records the user's walking activity response for risk assessment.

06

Previous Injury

Considers the user's reported history of previous foot injury.

07

Heel & Arch Symptoms

Includes reported heel and arch symptoms in the assessment inputs.

08

Health Factors

Includes swelling and diabetes-related fields in the assessment.

09

Random Forest

Uses a trained Random Forest classifier to generate a model-based prediction.
