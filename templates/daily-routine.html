Daily Care Routine

Follow a simple daily routine to support healthy feet and reduce plantar fasciitis risk.

1

Morning Check

Start the day by checking for heel or arch discomfort. Avoid sudden heavy activity if pain or stiffness is noticed.

2

Gentle Movement

Perform gentle foot and ankle movements before beginning regular walking or other daily activities.

3

Activity Breaks

If your routine involves prolonged standing or walking, take regular breaks and avoid staying in one position for too long.

4

Evening Care

Give your feet adequate rest after daily activities. Gentle stretching and comfortable positioning can support relaxation.
