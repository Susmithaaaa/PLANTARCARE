Plantar Care Guidance

General foot-care guidance to support healthy daily foot habits.

Daily Foot Care

  • Keep the feet clean and dry.
  • Check the feet regularly for discomfort or changes.
  • Avoid prolonged activities when discomfort increases.
  • Follow comfortable and safe daily movement habits.

Foot Support

  • Choose comfortable footwear with suitable support.
  • Avoid footwear that causes pressure or discomfort.
  • Use appropriate cushioning when recommended.
  • Replace worn-out footwear when necessary.

Activity Management

  • Introduce physical activity gradually.
  • Take short breaks during prolonged standing.
  • Use gentle stretching as appropriate.
  • Stop an activity if it causes significant discomfort.

Professional Guidance

PLANTARCARE provides an educational risk assessment and general care information. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

If foot pain is persistent, severe, or worsening, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
